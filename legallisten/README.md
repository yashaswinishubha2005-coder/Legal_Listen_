# LegalListen

Multilingual, voice-based, uncertainty-aware legal case understanding.

LegalListen converts informal and code-mixed spoken narratives into a structured
Legal Case Graph linking people, events, facts, claims, evidence and potential
legal issues. It separates user-reported, evidence-supported, inferred,
conflicting and unknown information, asks adaptive follow-up questions, and
escalates to a legal professional when uncertainty is high.
